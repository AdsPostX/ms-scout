<objective>
## Goal
Two distinct improvements that share no state and can ship in parallel:

1. **👀 reaction on @mention** — Replace `:thinking_face:` with `:eyes:` in the DM path,
   and add `:eyes:` to the channel-mention path (fires immediately on the user's message,
   drops in `finally:`). Single emoji, single lifecycle. No `:hourglass:` — zero
   information gain from a second spinner.

2. **Alert post state write** — After every successful MONITOR_ALARM `chat_postMessage`
   in `demand_feed_main.py`, write the message_ts + channel to `alert_registry.set_post_state()`.
   This is what lets PR 12-03's acknowledge/snooze handlers call `chat_update` on the correct
   message.

## Why this PR is second
Depends on `AlertPostState` from PR 12-01. Both changes touch different files and can be
executed simultaneously.

## Output
- `scout_handlers.py` — reaction emoji changed in DM path; eyes reaction added to channel path
- `demand_feed_main.py` — 3 post_state writes after MONITOR_ALARM posts
- `smoke_test.py` — 2 new guards
</objective>
